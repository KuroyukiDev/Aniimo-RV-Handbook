1. Homeland & RV Foundation

Milestone 01

Unlocking Your Mobile Pocket Dimension

The RV is far more than cosmetic housing—it acts as your account’s resource hub, passive incubator station, and passive income generator.

Step 1: Complete Sea of Flowers
Progress through main story quests until you report to Dr. Lewis and achieve Student Level II status.
Step 2: Meet Fannie
In your Elite Trainer tab, track "Prologue: Wondrous RV" to find Fannie’s camper on your map.
TRAINER NOTE — PARK BOARDS: You can summon and park your RV at designated Park Boards throughout Idyll. Entering the RV lets you step through the back door portal directly into your private Homeland farmstead without wasting open-world travel time.

Elemental Job Affinity Distribution

An Aniimo’s Element governs which specific Homeland stations it can operate. Matching the appropriate element ensures uninterrupted offline automated farming:

Grass Planting & Gathering

Crucial for sowing seeds in farm plots and gathering wild herbs.

Water Irrigation & Brewing

Waters crops to avoid dry stalls; required to brew tonics & drinks.

Earth Quarrying & Mining

Extracts stone, minerals, and base ore from homeland deposit mounds.

Wind Milling & Processing

Runs windmills, flour grinders, and carding brushes for fibers.

Fire / Spark Smelting & Cooking

Fuels bread ovens and cooks battle rations in the Homestead Kitchen.

Ice Cold Storage / Cooling

Extends perishability timers for cooked dishes and dairy yields.

Dark Harvesting & Drying

Harvests fully grown plots cleanly and dries herbs/meats on racks.

Light Incubation & Growth Aura

Illuminates the homestead and accelerates egg timers in Hatchinators.

THE "DO NOT RELEASE" RULE: When cleaning your box using bulk release, be careful not to trash evolved or rare-element variants that roll Level 2+ Work Traits. An Aniimo with terrible battle stats can still be an S-Tier Homeland worker!

3. Production Chains & Layout Meta

Optimization

The Multi-Tier Production Pipeline

Raw crops earn negligible Home Coins. Raw items should always be funneled through Tier 2 and Tier 3 processing lines before fulfilling daily orders or vendor sales:

Hatchinator Passive Synergy

Hatchinators are your primary source of high-potential Aniimo and rare variant traits.

  • Keep an active Light-element Aniimo assigned near your incubator zone to reduce egg hatch duration by up to 25%.
  • Never leave a slot empty before logging off; offline timers continue running while your game is closed.
  • Prioritize eggs obtained from the Egg Heist activity to unlock cross-trait workers.
